EMBER DUEL

A fiery tactical card duel

How to Play

  • Goal: Reduce rival mage Cinder's HP to 0 before she defeats you.
  • Turns: Each turn, play 1 card from your hand of 5. Cinder then plays her card.
  • Ember (Mana): Starts at 3, grows by +1 each turn (max 10). Cards cost 0–4 ember.
  • Card Types: Strikes (damage), Guards (block), Mends (heal), Surges (+ember), Phoenix (rare strike + heal).
  • Controls: Click cards or press keys 1–5. Press Space/Enter to start, M to mute.
